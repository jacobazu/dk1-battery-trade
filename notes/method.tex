{\sffamily\bfseries\Large DK1 battery trading: methods}\par
\href{https://github.com/jacobazu/dk1-battery-trade}{github.com/jacobazu/dk1-battery-trade} \textperiodcentered{} October 2026

A 2\,MWh / 1\,MW battery planned every day on my DK1 price forecast, with its risk, costs and P\&L. Python, checked against the R version.

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}lL@{}}
\toprule
Plan & Mixed-integer linear program on the price forecast: 90\% round trip, one cycle a day \citep{bradbury2014}. Solved with SciPy. \\
Risk & EWMA-filtered historical simulation of the forecast errors \citep{hull1998,riskmetrics1996}, a 5\% floor and the average below it, the Kupiec test \citep{kupiec1995}, and four stress tests. \\
Loss limit & A limit on the worst 5\% of scenarios \citep{rockafellar2000}. Tested and not used. \\
Costs & Nord Pool fee and an assumed wear cost in the plan; Energinet's tariffs as a side case. \\
Tests & Daily differences tested with a Newey--West variance \citep{newey1987}. \\
Rules & The plan only uses the forecast saved before the auction. Limits, cost cases and tests are set before running. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
